Privacy Policy

Understand how we process and safeguard your web browsing data.

1. Information and Tracking

We do not manage individual user accounts. General logs such as client IP addresses, browser types, and timestamp distributions are collected purely for server resource scaling, load optimization, and interface performance evaluations.

2. Advertising Infrastructure

We serve advertisements using Google AdSense. Google incorporates cookies to serve ads based on your previous browsing history across various digital properties. You can easily manage or opt out of these personalized ad configurations inside your local Google Ads Settings panel.

3. Third-Party Web Redirection

Our database contains external reference hyperlinks pointing directly to outside AI providers. Once clicked, you enter completely separate data parameters outside our scope. We advise checking the specific terms hosted on those third-party sites directly.

4. Revisions

This privacy declaration will be revised continuously as external advertising frameworks adapt or global tracking compliance standards change.
